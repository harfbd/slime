生命週期Life cycle

從一顆孢子，到一張正在延伸的網。From a tiny spore to a growing living network.

同一個生命，持續改變模樣。A life in changing forms.

以下以變形體型黏菌的典型有性生命週期為例。點選任一階段，或按「下一階段」循序閱讀。This is a simplified sexual life cycle of a plasmodial slime mold. Select a stage or follow the sequence.

孢子萌發Spore germination

孢子（Spore）在適合的環境萌發，形成微小的變形蟲狀細胞；有些會依水分條件呈現具鞭毛的型態。A spore germinates under suitable conditions. It produces a tiny amoeboid cell; a flagellated form may occur depending on water conditions.

相容細胞融合Compatible cells fuse

在典型有性生命週期中，交配型（Mating Types）相容的細胞融合，形成合子（Zygote）。交配型不等於人類的性別分類。In a typical sexual cycle, cells of compatible mating types fuse to form a zygote. Mating types should not be equated with human gender categories.

變形體生長Plasmodium grows

細胞核反覆分裂，細胞質仍連續相通，形成多核變形體（Plasmodium）。這就是常見會移動、會覓食的階段。Nuclei divide while the cytoplasm remains continuous. The resulting multinucleate plasmodium is the familiar moving, feeding stage.

形成子實體Fruiting & dispersal

在適當的環境訊號下，變形體可轉為形成子實體（Fruiting Bodies），產生並散布孢子，延續生命週期。Suitable environmental cues can trigger fruiting-body formation. Spores are produced and dispersed, continuing the cycle.

概念順序，並非等長的時間區間。A sequence of stages, not equal time intervals.

這些小小的構造，有什麼任務？Small structures, a different task.

變形體的重點是覓食與生長；子實體的重點則轉向繁殖與散布。把兩個階段分開看，就比較不會把所有黏菌都想成一團黃色黏液。The plasmodium feeds and grows; fruiting bodies shift the focus to reproduction and dispersal. Recognizing these roles reveals more than the familiar yellow blob.

照片展示另一種黏菌的子實體，並非上頁多頭絨泡黏菌的下一張連拍。不同物種的外觀與生命史細節會有差異。This photograph shows a different species, not the next frame of the Physarum photograph. Appearance and life-history details vary among species.

「融合」和「聚集」有什麼不同？Fusion is not the same as aggregation.
