認識黏菌Meet slime molds

先認識它是誰，再理解那些令人驚訝的行為。Begin with what these organisms are, before exploring what they can do.

本頁導覽On this page它是什麼Identity兩種生存方式Two lifestyles生活環境Habitats資料來源Sources

名字有「菌」，卻不是一般的真菌。The name can be misleading.

「黏菌」是一些具有相似生活樣貌的真核生物（Eukaryotes）的通稱，不是單一物種，也不是細菌。本站聚焦變形體型與細胞型黏菌。“Slime molds” is a common name for several kinds of eukaryotic organisms, not one species and not bacteria. Here we focus on plasmodial and cellular slime molds.

有細胞核的生物稱為真核生物。許多黏菌在營養階段會移動、吞噬微生物；形成子實體時又讓人聯想到菇類，因此曾被歸在真菌之中。Eukaryotic cells contain nuclei. Many slime molds move and engulf microbes while feeding, yet their fruiting structures can resemble fungi—one reason they were once classified with them.

閱讀來源Read the source · UC Berkeley · Introduction to the Slime Molds

兩種生存方式，先分清楚。Two ways of living.

變形體型黏菌Plasmodial slime molds

以多頭絨泡黏菌（Physarum polycephalum）為例。營養階段的變形體是連續的細胞質，內含許多細胞核。In Physarum polycephalum, the feeding plasmodium is a continuous mass of cytoplasm containing many nuclei.

細胞型黏菌Cellular slime molds

以盤基網柄菌（Dictyostelium discoideum）為例。平時各自生活，食物不足時聚集；細胞仍保留各自的界線。Dictyostelium discoideum lives as separate cells. When food runs short, cells aggregate but retain their own boundaries.

圖解是細胞關係示意，並非實際比例。聚集不會讓物種「失去學名」；也不能把所有黏菌都描述成一個巨大細胞。These diagrams show cell relationships, not actual scale. Aggregation does not erase a species’ scientific name, and not all slime molds form one giant cell.

它們生活在哪裡？A world close to the ground.

在潮濕的落葉、腐木與樹皮附近，能找到不同黏菌的蹤跡。它們常以細菌等微生物為食，參與土壤與森林微小生物之間的互動。Damp leaf litter, decaying wood and bark can shelter slime molds. Many feed on bacteria and other microbes, taking part in the small-scale interactions of forest and soil communities.

上圖是人工培養情境；自然環境中的黏菌不一定呈黃色，也不一定形成這樣清楚的網絡。The photograph shows a laboratory culture. Wild slime molds are not always yellow and do not always form such visible networks.
