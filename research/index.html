黏菌與科學Science & discovery

讓驚奇帶你走進科學，也讓證據幫你分辨比喻與結論。Let curiosity lead, and evidence keep the story grounded.

驚奇背後，先看研究怎麼做。Follow the experiments.

「很聰明」容易吸引注意；科學更在意的是：觀察到什麼？在什麼條件下？有沒有其他解釋？“Smart” catches our attention. Science asks what was observed, under which conditions, and what else might explain it.

2000

在迷宮中連接兩個食物點Connecting food through a maze

研究者觀察多頭絨泡黏菌在迷宮中重整身體，保留連接食物點的短路徑。這是特定實驗條件下的結果，並非黏菌理解了人類的迷宮規則。Researchers observed Physarum reorganizing in a maze to retain a short connection between food sites. The result does not mean it understands human maze rules.

閱讀來源Read the source · Nakagaki et al. (2000) · Maze-solving by an amoeboid organism
2010

把食物點當成城市Food locations stand in for cities

研究以食物點對應東京周邊城市，觀察黏菌形成的網絡。重點是理解建置成本、運送效率與容錯能力之間如何取捨。Food sites represented cities around Tokyo. The resulting networks helped researchers study trade-offs among construction cost, transport efficiency and resilience.

閱讀來源Read the source · Tero et al. (2010) · Rules for Biologically Inspired Adaptive Network Design
2012

把資訊留在走過的路上Information outside the body

研究將黏液痕跡與路徑選擇連起來。它提醒我們：記憶的功能可以由生物與環境共同完成，不一定只能討論大腦。Experiments connected slime trails with route choices, showing that a memory-like function can involve both an organism and its environment.

閱讀來源Read the source · Reid et al. (2012) · Externalized spatial memory
2016

細胞融合後，行為也會改變？Can behavior transfer through fusion?

研究報告：經過訓練、對鹽產生習慣化的黏菌，能在與未受訓個體融合後傳遞這種行為改變。這不等於交換人類式的故事或知識。Researchers reported that habituation to salt could transfer from trained to untrained Physarum through fusion. This is not equivalent to exchanging human stories or knowledge.

閱讀來源Read the source · Vogel & Dussutour (2016) · Direct transfer of learned behaviour via cell fusion

延伸影片：從黏菌到宇宙網。Beyond biology: a cosmic connection.

原網站收錄了 Anton Petrov 的這支影片。觀看時請分清楚：以黏菌行為為靈感的演算法（Algorithm），與真實生物本身，是不同層次的研究。This Anton Petrov video appeared on the original website. Distinguish an algorithm inspired by slime-mold behavior from experiments on the living organism itself.

思考題：如果食物點可以代表城市，其他領域中的「點」和「連線」還能代表什麼？請先提出類比，再查證是否有研究支持。A question to explore: if food sites can represent cities, what might nodes and connections represent elsewhere? Make an analogy, then look for evidence.

把「有趣」和「已證實」分開。Wonder with precision.

可以說What the evidence supports

在特定條件下，多頭絨泡黏菌能展現路徑調整、對刺激反應改變等行為。Under particular conditions, Physarum can reorganize paths and change its responses to stimuli.

不宜直接推論What does not follow

它擁有人類意識、理解城市交通，或能取代工程師做所有設計。This does not establish human consciousness, an understanding of city transport, or a replacement for all engineering design.

從觀察建立模型，再檢驗模型能否解釋現象；這也是把生物學連結到資訊科學的好起點。Build a model from observations, then test what it can explain. That is a useful starting point for connecting biology and computing.
