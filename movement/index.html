移動與覓食Movement & foraging

沒有中央指揮，身體本身也能感受環境、調整路徑。A connected body can respond to its surroundings without a central controller.

本頁導覽On this page身體裡的流動Internal flow探索與連線Exploring paths留在環境的資訊Slime trails資料來源Sources

先讓身體裡的物質動起來。Flow inside a living network.

多頭絨泡黏菌沒有心臟，卻能靠管道的節律性收縮，帶動細胞質往返流動。這稱為往返流動（Shuttle Streaming），有助於在相連的身體各處運送物質。Physarum has no heart. Rhythmic contractions drive cytoplasm back and forth through its tubes. This shuttle streaming transports material through the connected body.

01 / SHUTTLE STREAMING
亮點代表移動的物質；速度、尺寸及管道形狀皆為概念示意。Bright markers represent transported material. Speed, scale and tube shape are schematic.
閱讀來源Read the source · UC Berkeley · Introduction to the Slime Molds

先探索，再重整。Explore, connect, refine.

面對食物線索，變形體會改變生長方向與管道配置。某些連線被維持，另一些逐漸退縮；在實驗裡，這種動態調整能產生有效的食物連接路徑。Food cues influence growth and tube organization. Some connections remain while others shrink. In experiments, this dynamic process can create efficient connections between food sources.

A、B、C：食物位置A, B, C: food locations淡線：其他連線Faint lines: other connections

探索階段：身體延伸，形成多條可能的連線。Exploration: the organism spreads and forms multiple possible connections.

重整階段：部分管道維持或增粗，其他連線逐漸退縮。Refinement: some tubes remain or thicken while other connections recede.

這是概念動畫，不是生物模擬或實驗數據，也不保證黏菌在任何條件下都找到最短路徑。This is a conceptual animation, not a biological simulation or experimental data. It does not imply that slime molds always find the shortest route.

閱讀來源Read the source · Nakagaki et al. (2000) · Maze-solving by an amoeboid organism

走過的地方，也能留下資訊。A trail can hold information.

2012 年的研究發現，多頭絨泡黏菌會避開自己先前留下的胞外黏液。在研究條件下，這能協助它避開已探索區域，被稱為外部空間記憶（Externalized Spatial Memory）。A 2012 study found that Physarum avoids its own extracellular slime trails. Under the tested conditions, this helped it avoid previously explored areas—an example of externalized spatial memory.

可以想成「在環境留下路標」。這個比喻幫助理解，但不代表黏菌像人一樣記得一段經歷。Think of it as leaving information in the environment. That analogy is helpful, but it does not establish a human-like recollection of experience.

閱讀來源Read the source · Reid et al. (2012) · Externalized spatial memory
